What PinViddl Is

PinViddl is a free online tool for checking public Pinterest video, image, and animated Pins. Paste one Pin URL, and the service will try to find media that can be previewed or downloaded to your device.

The interface runs in your browser, but link processing uses a PinViddl backend. You do not need to create a PinViddl account or provide an email address to use the downloader. Private, removed, restricted, or unsupported Pins may not work, even if a link opens for you on Pinterest.

PinViddl is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Pinterest, Inc. in any way. The name "Pinterest" is used purely to describe what the tool works with — processing Pinterest Pin URLs to retrieve the media attached to them.

Why It Exists

Sometimes you want a copy of a public Pin for offline viewing or personal reference, but finding the right file and saving it can take extra steps. PinViddl gives you a simple place to check what media is available from a single Pin link.

The workflow is straightforward: paste a public Pin URL, review the media options the service can identify, then use the download button or copy the media URL. Availability and quality depend on the source Pin and what Pinterest makes accessible at the time.

Video, image, and GIF pages help you find the relevant instructions and tool. The service may not be able to retrieve every Pin, and it does not convert, upscale, or repair source media.

How It Works

When you submit a Pin URL, your browser sends it to a PinViddl processing endpoint. The backend attempts to read the public Pin and identify media URLs. Preview and download requests are then routed through the service infrastructure, which relays the selected file to your browser.

PinViddl does not ask for your Pinterest password and cannot unlock private or restricted content. The Pin URL is transmitted to the processing service; infrastructure providers may process request data as described in the Privacy Policy. Do not submit links or information you consider sensitive.

Three Tools, One Purpose

Pinterest hosts three types of media, and each one requires a different retrieval approach. PinViddl splits them into three dedicated downloaders so each one is optimised for its format rather than trying to handle everything through a single fallback path.

Pinterest Video Downloader Checks a public video Pin and shows a video download option when one is available.
Pinterest Image Downloader Checks a public photo Pin and offers an image file when one is available.
Pinterest GIF Downloader Checks an animated Pin and offers the media format identified by the service; it does not convert formats.

What PinViddl Doesn't Do

Being clear about what a tool doesn't do is as important as listing what it does.

No PinViddl account is required. Submitted URLs are sent to the processing endpoint to retrieve a Pin. Service providers may handle request data under their own infrastructure practices; see the Privacy Policy for details.

It doesn't access private content. PinViddl only reads publicly accessible Pinterest pages. Private boards, login-restricted Pins, and deleted content are outside its reach by design.

It is not Pinterest. PinViddl is an independent service. Its backend and delivery infrastructure may relay media between Pinterest and your browser.

It doesn't require a Pinterest account. PinViddl doesn't log in to Pinterest on your behalf and doesn't ask for your credentials under any circumstances.

It uses third-party services. The site loads Google Analytics and third-party advertising code. These providers may collect information through their scripts; review the Privacy Policy and provider policies for details.

It doesn't download entire boards. PinViddl is designed for one Pin URL per request, not for downloading entire boards.

Features

Video as MP4

When a compatible video is found, the tool provides the available video file. The source format and quality depend on the Pin and Pinterest’s response.

Image Downloads

When an image file is available, you can preview it and save the version returned by the service. The tool does not upscale the source image.

GIF & Animated Pins

Animated Pins are offered in the format returned for that Pin. Some may be provided as video rather than as a .gif file.

Media Preview

Before downloading, a live preview of the video, image, or animation is shown so you can confirm it's the right file.

Request Status

The page shows a loading indicator while it checks the Pin. Processing time can vary with Pinterest and network response.

Mobile-Optimised

The layout adapts to smaller screens. Clipboard access depends on browser permissions and device support.
