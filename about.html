What PinViddl Is

PinViddl is a free, browser-based tool for downloading publicly available Pinterest content. You paste the URL of a Pinterest Pin — a video Pin, a photo Pin, or an animated GIF Pin — and PinViddl processes it and returns the media file for you to save to your device.

It runs entirely in your browser. There is no app to download, no account to create, no email address to hand over. You don't need a Pinterest account either. If the Pin is publicly visible on Pinterest, PinViddl can retrieve it.

PinViddl is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Pinterest, Inc. in any way. The name "Pinterest" is used purely to describe what the tool works with — processing Pinterest Pin URLs to retrieve the media attached to them.

Why It Exists

Pinterest is a discovery platform — it's built for browsing and collecting ideas, not for saving media to your device. Pinterest doesn't offer a native download button for videos, and right-clicking images on the platform typically saves a compressed thumbnail rather than the full-resolution original. Saving content for offline use, personal reference, or creative work is unnecessarily hard.

PinViddl was built to solve that specific friction. The goal is a tool that gets out of your way: paste a link, get the file, done. No upsell to a premium tier, no "download limit reached" message after five files, no request for payment details to access HD quality.

That philosophy hasn't changed since the first version. Everything added since launch — media preview before downloading, live progress tracking, a dedicated image downloader, a dedicated GIF downloader — has been built around the same principle: make saving Pinterest content as direct as possible.

How It Works

PinViddl reads the public Pinterest page behind any Pin URL you provide — the same page your browser would load if you visited the link directly. It extracts the media URL embedded in that page and returns the actual file to your browser for download.

It doesn't log in to Pinterest on your behalf, access private content, or store anything you submit. The Pin URL is processed in memory for the duration of the request and discarded immediately after. Nothing is written to disk.

Three Tools, One Purpose

Pinterest hosts three types of media, and each one requires a different retrieval approach. PinViddl splits them into three dedicated downloaders so each one is optimised for its format rather than trying to handle everything through a single fallback path.

Pinterest Video Downloader Saves video Pins as MP4 files at the highest available resolution.
Pinterest Image Downloader Retrieves the full-resolution image from any public photo Pin.
Pinterest GIF Downloader Downloads animated Pins — handles Pinterest's GIF-to-MP4 conversion automatically.

What PinViddl Doesn't Do

Being clear about what a tool doesn't do is as important as listing what it does.

It doesn't store your Pin URLs. URLs you submit are processed in memory and discarded the moment the response is sent. Nothing is logged or written to a database.

It doesn't access private content. PinViddl only reads publicly accessible Pinterest pages. Private boards, login-restricted Pins, and deleted content are outside its reach by design.

It doesn't host or serve media files. Media files stay on Pinterest's servers. PinViddl retrieves the direct URL and your browser downloads the file from Pinterest directly, not from our infrastructure.

It doesn't require a Pinterest account. PinViddl doesn't log in to Pinterest on your behalf and doesn't ask for your credentials under any circumstances.

It doesn't run ads that track you. PinViddl does not integrate advertising networks, retargeting pixels, or social media tracking scripts on its pages.

It doesn't download entire boards. PinViddl processes one Pin URL at a time and is not designed for bulk or automated content extraction.

Features

Video as MP4

Pinterest video Pins are downloaded as clean MP4 files at the highest resolution Pinterest makes available for that Pin.

Full-Resolution Images

Image Pins are retrieved at full source resolution — not the compressed thumbnail Pinterest shows in feeds.

GIF & Animated Pins

Animated Pins are downloaded in the format Pinterest stores them — usually MP4, which plays identically to the original GIF animation.

Media Preview

Before downloading, a live preview of the video, image, or animation is shown so you can confirm it's the right file.

Live Progress Tracking

A real-time progress bar and percentage indicator shows processing status while the Pin is being retrieved.

Mobile-Optimised

The interface is designed for mobile use — large touch targets, responsive layout, and clipboard paste support in mobile browsers.
