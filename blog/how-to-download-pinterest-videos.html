How to Download Pinterest Videos in HD — Complete Guide

Pinterest doesn't include a save button for videos. This guide covers exactly how to save any public Pinterest video to your phone or computer using PinViddl — no app install, no Pinterest account, and no quality loss.

September 10, 2026 6 min read PinViddl Team

Why Pinterest Has No Download Button

Pinterest is built around discovery and saving ideas to boards — not around exporting files to your device. The platform deliberately omits a video download button. That's fine for in-app browsing, but it's a problem when you need offline access: a workout video to follow without a data connection, a cooking tutorial for later, a design reference to keep locally.

PinViddl solves that gap. It reads the public Pinterest page behind any Pin URL you provide and extracts the video stream embedded in it — the same stream your browser uses to play the video on Pinterest.

Important

PinViddl only works with publicly accessible Pins — content anyone can view without logging in to Pinterest. Private boards and login-restricted Pins cannot be processed by any external tool.

What You Need

The full list is short:

  • The URL of a Pinterest video Pin
  • Any modern browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge on phone or desktop
  • Nothing else — no app, no Pinterest account, no sign-up

Finding the Right Pinterest URL

The most common mistake is pasting the wrong link. PinViddl needs the URL of the individual Pin, not a board, profile, or search page.

A valid Pin URL looks like one of these:

  • https://www.pinterest.com/pin/123456789012345678/
  • https://pin.it/AbCdEfG — shortened share link from the app

A board URL — which won't work — looks like: https://www.pinterest.com/username/board-name/

Tip

If you're inside a board and see a video you want, tap the Pin to open it as its own full page first. The URL should then contain /pin/ followed by a number — that's the one you need.

Step-by-Step: How to Download a Pinterest Video

1
Open the Pin on Pinterest

Find the video Pin in the app or browser. Make sure you've opened the Pin itself — not just scrolled past it in a feed or board.

2
Copy the Pin URL

In the Pinterest app: tap the Share icon then select Copy Link. In a browser: copy directly from the address bar.

3
Open PinViddl

Go to pinviddl.com in your browser. The input field is the first thing on the page.

4
Paste the URL and hit Download

Tap the Paste button or paste manually, then click Download. A live progress bar shows processing status.

5
Preview and save

A video preview appears with download options. Click Download next to your preferred quality — the MP4 saves directly to your device.

Where Your File Saves — By Device

The download steps are the same on every device, but where the file lands varies:

DeviceBrowserWhere it saves
AndroidChromeDownloads folder; usually appears in Gallery automatically
iPhone / iPadSafariFiles app → Downloads; tap Share → Save Video to move to Camera Roll
iPhone / iPadChromeVaries — usually prompts to open in Files or share to Photos
WindowsAnyC:\Users\[you]\Downloads
MacAny~/Downloads

iPhone note

Safari on iOS downloads to the Files app by default. After the download completes a banner appears at the top — tap it to open the file. To move it to your Camera Roll, tap Share inside Files and choose Save Video.

Android note

Chrome drops MP4s directly into your Downloads folder. Most gallery apps scan Downloads automatically so the video should appear within seconds. If it doesn't, open your Files app and check the Downloads folder.

Video Quality

PinViddl retrieves the video at whatever resolution Pinterest is serving for that Pin — typically 720p or 1080p for recent uploads. If multiple quality options are detected they appear as separate choices. The file is not re-encoded or compressed; you get exactly what Pinterest is serving.

Quality note

Quality is limited by what was uploaded to Pinterest originally. PinViddl cannot upscale a video that was uploaded at low resolution.

When the Download Doesn't Work

  • Wrong URL type. The link points to a board, profile, or search page instead of a Pin. Open the Pin itself — the URL must contain /pin/.
  • Private or deleted Pin. If Pinterest requires a login to view it, or the Pin has been removed, no downloader can access it.
  • It's a GIF, not a video. Short silent loops are stored differently. Try the Pinterest GIF downloader.
  • Pinterest updated its structure. Pinterest occasionally changes how it delivers content. If a valid link isn't working, try again in a few hours.
  • URL was cut off. Some apps truncate long URLs on copy. Re-copy using the share menu's Copy Link option.

Copyright and Responsible Use

Every Pinterest video belongs to the person who uploaded it. Saving a copy for personal offline reference — a recipe, a workout, a tutorial — is the intended use case for PinViddl.

Reuploading, using in commercial content, or presenting someone else's video as your own requires explicit permission from the rights holder. See PinViddl's Disclaimer for the full picture.
