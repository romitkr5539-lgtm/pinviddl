How to Download Pinterest Videos: Phone and Computer Guide

Want to watch a video Pin offline? Start with Pinterest’s own download option when it is available. If you have permission to save the Pin and that option is not offered, this guide explains the PinViddl workflow, where downloads go, and what to try when a link fails.

Updated October 27, 20267 min readPinViddl

Check Pinterest’s download option first

Some Pinterest video Pins can be downloaded directly in Pinterest. Whether the option appears depends on the Pin and its creator’s settings. Pinterest says creators can allow downloads for full-screen video Pins; when that option is enabled, the downloaded video may include the creator’s username as a watermark. Look for a download option in the Pin’s menu before using another method.

If Pinterest does not show a download action, that may be intentional or the Pin may not be eligible. Saving a Pin to a board keeps it in your Pinterest account; it does not necessarily save a video file to your device.

Good to know

A public link does not mean the creator has granted permission to reuse the video. Keep a personal copy only when you have the right to do so, and ask the creator before reposting or using it in a project.

What you need

  • A link to one individual, publicly viewable video Pin. A board, profile, or search-results link is not the same thing.
  • A browser on your phone or computer.
  • Permission to save the video for your intended use.

To copy a link in Pinterest, open the Pin and use its share option, then choose Copy link. On desktop, you can also copy the address from the browser after opening the Pin itself.

Use a Pin link with PinViddl

  1. Open the Pinterest Downloader in your browser.
  2. Paste the individual Pin URL into the input field.
  3. Submit the link and wait while the service checks for available media.
  4. If a result appears, preview it when possible and choose its download action.
  5. Confirm the browser’s save prompt, or check its Downloads list if the file starts automatically.

Save the file on your device

The browser controls the final save location, so the exact prompt can vary with your settings.

DeviceWhere to look
iPhone or iPadCheck Safari’s Downloads list or the Downloads folder in the Files app. Use the share menu from Files if you want to move a compatible video to Photos.
Android phone or tabletCheck the browser’s download notification or the Downloads folder in your Files app. Gallery visibility depends on the device.
Windows PCCheck the browser’s downloads panel or your configured Downloads folder.
MacCheck the browser’s downloads list or the Downloads folder in Finder.

What to expect from video quality

The file you receive depends on what the Pin and Pinterest make available to the processing service. PinViddl does not upscale or improve a source video, and it may not expose every quality version. A smaller or softer-looking result can reflect the source file, the version Pinterest serves, or how the video is encoded.

If you need a particular resolution, compare the downloaded file with the original Pin and avoid assuming that a downloader can restore detail that was not present in the source.

Fix common download problems

  • The page rejects the link: open the Pin itself and copy its link again. Board, profile, and search URLs are not individual Pin URLs.
  • No media result appears: the Pin may be private, removed, restricted, or temporarily unavailable. Try a different public Pin to tell whether the issue is link-specific.
  • The link is shortened: a Pinterest share link may redirect to the Pin. If it fails, open it in Pinterest or a browser and copy the final Pin address.
  • The download does not start: check browser download permissions, available device storage, pop-up or content blockers, and the browser’s download list.
  • The result is not a video: the Pin may contain an image or animated file instead. Use the matching image or GIF and animated Pin guide.

Use the video responsibly

Downloading a copy does not transfer copyright or other rights. Do not repost, sell, edit into another work, or use a creator’s video commercially unless you have the necessary permission or licence. For platform-specific options and permissions, use Pinterest’s controls and guidance.

Official reference
