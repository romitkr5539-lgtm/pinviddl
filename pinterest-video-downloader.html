Download a Pinterest video in three steps

1

On Pinterest, open the video Pin and choose Share → Copy link. You can also copy the Pin URL from your browser’s address bar.

2

Paste the link into the box above and select Download. PinViddl checks that it points to a Pin and looks for an available video file.

3

When a VIDEO Option row appears, choose Download Video to save it or Copy URL to copy the direct media address.

What the video results show

For a public video Pin, PinViddl lists the video files it can retrieve as VIDEO Option rows. Each row has a Download Video button and a Copy URL button. Download saves that option through your browser; Copy URL copies the direct media address, not the Pinterest post link.

When Pinterest returns more than one usable video file, review the rows before choosing. PinViddl does not invent higher resolution or sharpen a source. If no quality value is available, “Super HD Source” may appear as a fallback label; it does not tell you the file’s pixel dimensions.

Copy the URL of one public video Pin

Open the video Pin itself and use Pinterest’s Share → Copy link option. You can also copy the address bar after the Pin opens on its own page. A full Pin URL or a valid pin.it share link can work.

Links to boards, profiles, search pages, or the home feed are not individual Pin links. Private, removed, or sign-in-only Pins cannot be retrieved.

The preview can fail while the file link still works

PinViddl loads the video preview and the download rows separately. If the preview area says “Preview unavailable — use the Download button below” and a video result is listed, try Download Video.

If the page reports “No downloadable media found for this Pin,” the public Pin did not return a usable video file. Check that you pasted the video Pin itself, not its board or profile.

Find the saved video on your device

  • Android: check your browser’s Downloads list or the Downloads folder in your file manager.
  • iPhone or iPad: check Safari Downloads or Files → Downloads. From Files, use Share to save the video to Photos if available.
  • Windows or Mac: look in the browser’s download list or Downloads folder. If the video opens in a tab, use the browser’s save option.

When a Pinterest video link is not working

Check that the link opens one public video Pin, the Pin has not been deleted, and the URL was copied in full. A short pin.it link may stop working; open it and copy the full Pin address to try again.

If the same public Pin keeps failing, send its link and the exact message you see through the Contact page.

Need an image or animated Pin instead?

Use the Pinterest Image Downloader for a still photo or graphic. For an animated Pin, use the Pinterest GIF Downloader; the result can be a GIF or an MP4 depending on the media Pinterest provides.

Use saved videos responsibly

Download videos you own or have permission to save. Ask the creator before reposting, editing, or using a video commercially. PinViddl is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Pinterest; see the Disclaimer and Privacy Policy.
