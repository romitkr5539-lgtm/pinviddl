Permitted & Prohibited Use

PinViddl is built for personal, non-commercial use. The following illustrates the boundary between acceptable and unacceptable use:

✓ Permitted

  • Saving a recipe video for offline personal reference
  • Downloading a wallpaper image for personal device use
  • Saving a workout clip to watch later without internet
  • Downloading a design reference image for personal mood-boarding
  • Saving a GIF animation for personal entertainment
  • Downloading your own content that you uploaded to Pinterest

✗ Prohibited

  • Reposting downloaded content on social media without credit or permission
  • Using downloaded media in commercial projects or advertising
  • Bulk or automated downloading of content (scraping)
  • Downloading private or login-restricted content
  • Using downloaded content to train AI or machine learning models
  • Any use that violates Pinterest's Terms of Service or applicable copyright law

When in doubt about whether a specific use is permissible, contact the content creator directly before proceeding. This is the safest approach regardless of which download tool you use.
