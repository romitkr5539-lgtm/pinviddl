01

Overview

PinViddl ("we", "us", "our") operates pinviddl.com — a free, browser-based tool that lets users download publicly available Pinterest media including videos, images, and GIFs. This Privacy Policy describes information handled by the site and its service providers, why it is used, and how to find out more about provider practices and your available choices.

You can use the downloader without creating a PinViddl account or entering contact details. To process a request, the Pinterest URL is sent to the service infrastructure. This site also loads Google Analytics and third-party advertising code, so data may be processed by those providers when their scripts run. We do not sell information you submit through the contact form.

This policy describes the site’s current practices and the third-party services visible in its pages. Use of the service remains subject to applicable laws and the separate terms of the providers involved.

02

What We Collect

The information we collect falls into two categories: data you actively provide and data collected automatically as you use the service.

2a. Data You Provide

Pinterest Pin URLs. When you submit a link, your browser sends it to a PinViddl processing endpoint so the service can try to identify the public Pin media. The request may pass through third-party infrastructure providers. We do not require a PinViddl account, but we cannot promise that every provider discards request data immediately; their handling is governed by their own systems and policies.

Contact form submissions. If you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, selected subject, and the message you write. This data is used only to respond to your enquiry and is handled via Formsubmit.co (see Third-Party Services below).

2b. Data Collected Automatically

Data type What it includes Why it is collected
Server log data Request metadata such as IP address, timestamp, requested path, response status, browser details, or referrer may be processed by hosting and infrastructure providers. Security monitoring, abuse prevention, and debugging errors
Analytics data Google Analytics may receive page and usage events, device/browser details, approximate location, and identifiers or cookie data, depending on the tag and account settings. Analytics and reporting, subject to Google Analytics configuration and policies.
Provider diagnostics Hosting, analytics, or ad providers may process technical request and performance signals as part of their services. Provider operations, measurement, security, or service delivery, depending on their settings

We cannot guarantee that analytics or infrastructure data is anonymous or that it cannot be associated with a device or browser. We do not control provider-side processing or account settings. See the relevant provider policy for more information.

03

How We Use Your Information

The data we collect is used only for the following purposes:

  • Processing download requests. A submitted Pin URL is sent to the processing service to locate media associated with a public Pin and relay a result to your browser.
  • Responding to contact enquiries. Name and email data from contact form submissions is used exclusively to reply to the message you sent.
  • Security and abuse prevention. Server log data (including IP addresses) is used to detect and block malicious activity, excessive automated requests, and other abuse of the service.
  • Measurement and advertising. Google Analytics and third-party advertising scripts are loaded on the site. Their providers may process information for measurement, ad delivery, fraud prevention, or related purposes under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Legal compliance. We may process data to the extent required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, or lawful requests from competent authorities.

Because third-party advertising code is present, we cannot state that advertising providers do not use cookies, identifiers, or other information for ad-related purposes. We do not control how those providers use data collected by their scripts.

04

Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be set by third-party analytics and advertising scripts loaded on this site. Their names, purposes, and lifetimes can depend on provider configuration and your browser or region.

Site and infrastructure cookies

The downloader pages do not appear to set a first-party cookie for the core tool. Hosting or security providers may use their own technologies. We cannot list or control cookies set by third-party scripts in every browser or configuration.

Analytics cookies

Google Analytics is included on site pages. Google may use cookies or similar identifiers for measurement according to the tag configuration and Google’s policies. The site code alone does not establish the exact data retention or privacy settings of the Analytics account.

Managing cookies

You can review, block, or delete cookies using your browser settings. Blocking third-party scripts or cookies may reduce analytics or advertising functionality; it may also affect features supplied by those providers. The site does not currently show a cookie preference panel.

For instructions on managing cookies in common browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Safari → Privacy & Security
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data
05

Third-Party Services

Third-party scripts and infrastructure can receive information when your browser connects to them. Their access and use are governed by their own policies; PinViddl does not control their systems or settings.

Cloudflare Workers (Media Processing)

The downloader sends Pin links and media requests to Cloudflare Worker endpoints. Cloudflare processes those requests as infrastructure provider and may process connection and request data. The site owner does not control Cloudflare’s independent logging or retention settings. Read Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Formsubmit.co (Contact Form)

When you submit the contact form, Formsubmit.co receives the fields you enter and forwards them to the site contact email. The provider may process request metadata as well as the form contents. Read Formsubmit’s privacy information. Messages received by email may remain in the inbox until deleted.

Google Analytics

Pages load Google Analytics through Google’s tag script using measurement ID G-D28LBZ52GX. Google Analytics can process page views and related usage, browser/device information, and identifiers according to the tag and property settings. This page cannot verify those private account settings, including advertising features or retention period. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Analytics data-retention documentation. You can also use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Content Delivery (CDN)

Site assets and requests may be delivered through hosting and content-delivery infrastructure. These providers necessarily receive connection information such as an IP address and request metadata; their handling is described in their own privacy notices.

The site loads advertising code from highrevenueformat.com. This provider or its partners may process information such as IP address, browser/device data, page context, and advertising identifiers to deliver or measure ads and prevent abuse. The site owner does not control the provider’s data collection, cookies, partners, or retention settings. Review the ad provider’s privacy notice and your browser controls for current details.

Data Retention

Retention varies by data type and provider. The table summarizes what PinViddl can confirm and where the period is controlled by an external provider.

Data type Retention period Reason
Pin URLs submitted for processing Used to process the request; provider-side retention is controlled by the infrastructure provider Used to identify and process the requested Pin
Server log data (IP, timestamp, path) Provider-specific; see the relevant service privacy terms and settings Security investigation and abuse detection
Google Analytics data Set by Google Analytics property settings; not verified by this site Long-term trend analysis for service improvement

PinViddl cannot independently verify or set retention periods for data held by Cloudflare, Google Analytics, Formsubmit.co, or advertising partners. Contact the relevant provider for information about its retention practices. Contact form emails can be deleted by the site operator after the enquiry is handled.

07

Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. These vary by jurisdiction but commonly include:

  • Right of access. You may request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification. If personal data we hold is inaccurate, you may request correction.
  • Right to erasure. You may request deletion of personal data we hold about you, where no legal obligation requires its retention.
  • Right to object. You may object to certain types of processing, including processing for direct marketing (though we do not engage in this).
  • Right to data portability. Where applicable, you may request personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Consent choices. Where a provider offers consent or opt-out controls, use those controls or your browser settings. This site currently does not display a cookie preference panel.

Requests about contact form messages or information the site operator can access may be sent through the contact page. We will review requests and respond as soon as practical. Data held by independent providers may need to be requested from those providers directly.

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom and believe we have not handled your data lawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

08

Security

Pages on PinViddl are served over HTTPS, which encrypts data in transit between your browser and the site. The underlying hosting, analytics, advertising, and media-processing providers operate their own security controls under their respective policies.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. While we take reasonable precautions, we cannot warrant that unauthorised third parties will never circumvent our security measures. We recommend using a modern, up-to-date browser and not sharing sensitive personal information through any web service unnecessarily.

If you discover a security vulnerability in PinViddl, please report it responsibly by contacting us through the contact page before any public disclosure.

09

Children's Privacy

PinViddl is not directed at or intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If a parent or guardian believes their child has submitted personal information to us — for example through the contact form — please contact us immediately at the address on our Contact page. We will delete the relevant data promptly upon verification.
