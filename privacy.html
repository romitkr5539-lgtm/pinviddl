01

Overview

PinViddl ("we", "us", "our") operates pinviddl.com — a free, browser-based tool that lets users download publicly available Pinterest media including videos, images, and GIFs. This Privacy Policy describes the personal and non-personal information we collect when you use PinViddl, the purposes for which that information is used, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights available to you.

PinViddl is designed to collect as little data as possible. The core function of the tool — processing a Pinterest URL and returning the associated media — does not require you to create an account, provide your name, or supply any contact information. We do not sell personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

By using PinViddl you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the service. Continued use after any update to this policy constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

02

What We Collect

The information we collect falls into two categories: data you actively provide and data collected automatically as you use the service.

2a. Data You Provide

Pinterest Pin URLs. When you paste a Pinterest link into the downloader, that URL is transmitted to our processing infrastructure solely to retrieve the associated media. We do not permanently store Pin URLs. They are processed in memory for the duration of the request and discarded immediately after the response is returned to your browser.

Contact form submissions. If you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, selected subject, and the message you write. This data is used only to respond to your enquiry and is handled via Formsubmit.co (see Third-Party Services below).

2b. Data Collected Automatically

Data type What it includes Why it is collected
Server log data IP address, request timestamp, HTTP method, URL path, response status code, browser user-agent, referring URL Security monitoring, abuse prevention, and debugging errors
Analytics data Pages visited, time on page, approximate geographic region (country/city level), device type, browser type, operating system Understanding aggregate usage patterns to improve the service
Performance data Page load times, error rates, API response times Monitoring and maintaining service stability

Analytics and performance data is collected in aggregate and anonymised form — it cannot be used to identify individual users. IP addresses in server logs are not cross-referenced with analytics data.

03

How We Use Your Information

The data we collect is used only for the following purposes:

  • Processing download requests. Pin URLs are used in real time to retrieve media from Pinterest's public pages and return it to you. No other use is made of these URLs.
  • Responding to contact enquiries. Name and email data from contact form submissions is used exclusively to reply to the message you sent.
  • Security and abuse prevention. Server log data (including IP addresses) is used to detect and block malicious activity, excessive automated requests, and other abuse of the service.
  • Service improvement. Anonymised analytics data is used to identify which features are used most, which pages load slowly, and where errors occur — information that informs development priorities.
  • Legal compliance. We may process data to the extent required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, or lawful requests from competent authorities.

We do not use your data for behavioural advertising. We do not build individual user profiles. We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade personal data to any third party for their own marketing purposes.

04

Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. PinViddl uses a minimal set of cookies:

Essential cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function. They do not track you across other websites and cannot be disabled without breaking core functionality. Examples include session identifiers used temporarily during a download request.

Analytics cookies

If third-party analytics are active (see section 5), analytics providers may set their own cookies to distinguish unique visitors and sessions. These cookies collect anonymised, aggregated data only. They do not store names, email addresses, or any directly identifying information.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block third-party cookies specifically while allowing essential first-party cookies. Blocking all cookies may affect the display or functionality of some parts of the site, but the core download tool will continue to work without analytics cookies present.

For instructions on managing cookies in common browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Safari → Privacy & Security
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data
05

Third-Party Services

PinViddl uses a small number of third-party services to operate the site. Each has access only to the data necessary for its specific function.

Cloudflare Workers (Media Processing)

Our media extraction backend runs on Cloudflare Workers. When you submit a Pin URL, it is sent to a Cloudflare Worker endpoint which fetches the relevant public Pinterest page and extracts the media URL. Cloudflare may log basic request metadata (IP address, timestamp, response code) as part of its standard infrastructure logging. Cloudflare's privacy practices are described at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Formsubmit.co (Contact Form)

Contact form submissions are processed by Formsubmit.co, which forwards them to our support email address. Formsubmit receives the name, email, subject, and message you submit. Their privacy policy is available at formsubmit.co. We do not store contact form data on our own servers beyond what arrives in our email inbox.

Analytics (Optional)

We may use an analytics service such as Google Analytics, Plausible Analytics, or a similar privacy-friendly alternative to understand aggregate traffic patterns. If Google Analytics is in use, it operates with IP anonymisation enabled and interest-based advertising features disabled. Anonymised analytics data is not shared with Google for advertising purposes. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Content Delivery (CDN)

Static assets (CSS, JavaScript, images) may be served through a content delivery network for performance. CDN providers may log standard request metadata as described in their respective privacy policies. No personal data is shared with CDN providers beyond what is inherent in the HTTP request (IP address, browser user-agent).

We do not integrate advertising networks, social media tracking pixels, or affiliate tracking technologies on PinViddl.

Data Retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for its stated purpose:

Data type Retention period Reason
Pin URLs submitted for processing Not stored — discarded after response Not needed beyond the immediate request
Server log data (IP, timestamp, path) Up to 30 days Security investigation and abuse detection
Anonymised analytics data Up to 26 months Long-term trend analysis for service improvement
Contact form submissions (email inbox) Until the enquiry is resolved, then deleted Responding to support requests

Server logs older than 30 days are automatically purged. We do not archive or back up personal data beyond the periods stated above.

Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. These vary by jurisdiction but commonly include:

  • Right of access. You may request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification. If personal data we hold is inaccurate, you may request correction.
  • Right to erasure. You may request deletion of personal data we hold about you, where no legal obligation requires its retention.
  • Right to object. You may object to certain types of processing, including processing for direct marketing (though we do not engage in this).
  • Right to data portability. Where applicable, you may request personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to withdraw consent. Where processing is based on consent (e.g., analytics cookies), you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.

Given that PinViddl collects minimal personal data and does not store Pin URLs, most data subject requests can be resolved quickly. Requests relating to contact form data or server logs can be directed to us via the contact page. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom and believe we have not handled your data lawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Security

PinViddl is served exclusively over HTTPS, which encrypts data in transit between your browser and our servers. We apply standard security practices to our infrastructure, including access controls, regular dependency updates, and monitoring for unusual traffic patterns.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. While we take reasonable precautions, we cannot warrant that unauthorised third parties will never circumvent our security measures. We recommend using a modern, up-to-date browser and not sharing sensitive personal information through any web service unnecessarily.

If you discover a security vulnerability in PinViddl, please report it responsibly by contacting us through the contact page before any public disclosure.

Children's Privacy

PinViddl is not directed at or intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If a parent or guardian believes their child has submitted personal information to us — for example through the contact form — please contact us immediately at the address on our Contact page. We will delete the relevant data promptly upon verification.
