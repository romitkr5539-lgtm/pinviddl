Save Pinterest Videos Easily with PinViddl

You have come across a video on Pinterest — maybe a recipe, a tutorial, or a clip that you would like to easily download and save. PinViddl serves a particular purpose by allowing you to copy and paste the Pin's URL to obtain a download link for an accessible video. The service does not require the creation of an account, a program installation, or a browser extension.

Only public Pins are supported. If the Pin is private or unavailable, it would not be possible to download it as there would be nothing for PinViddl to retrieve.

Download Pinterest Videos Online

The entire process takes place in a web browser. There is no need to download any additional software for your mobile device or computer, nor do you require a PinViddl account.

The only thing that PinViddl needs from its user is the link of the image in the Pin — the exact URL of the video in the Pin. Once you paste it there, PinViddl scans the Pin and checks for the presence of a video. If there is a video in the Pin, it will provide a link to download it. If there is no video (the Pin is a simple image, the video is deleted or the Pin no longer exists) there will be nothing for PinViddl to download and will just inform you about that, rather than lying about it.

How to Download a Pinterest Video

  1. Locate the video on Pinterest. Open the Pin like you would normally do it in your Pinterest app or on the Pinterest webpage.
  2. Get the Pin URL. Press on the share button on the Pin and tap "Copy link" or copy the full website address of the specific video from your browser.
  3. Paste it into PinViddl. Go to the PinViddl page and paste the link in the text box at the homepage.
  4. Start the download. PinViddl scans the Pin and searches for a video file available for download from it.
  5. Save the video. If the video file was found, PinViddl will give you a link to download it.

That's the full flow — no extra steps, no forced sign-ups in the middle of it.

How to Download Pinterest Videos on Mobile Devices

The procedure of downloading videos from Pinterest on a mobile device mirrors that of a computer, except that the end location of the download would differ depending on mobile operating system.

If you are using an Android OS device, you first need to open the Pin from within the Pinterest app, tap the share button followed by the Copy link option. Launch PinViddl on your mobile browser, paste the link, and commence download. Usually, on Android, the video will be stored automatically into the Downloads folder on the device and you can later transfer it to Gallery if you wish.

If you own an iPhone, the above steps remain the same up until the download. However, iOS saves the downloads differently. Safari saves the downloaded videos in the Files app, categorized under Downloads folder — hence they won't show up in the Photos app. To access the video in the Photos app, you need to access it from Files app and use the Share feature to save it.

Supported Pinterest URLs

Not every URL containing the term "pinterest.com" will lead exactly to a downloadable video, making many downloaders mislead. To begin with, there are different types of URLs:

  • Individual Pin URL (e.g. pinterest.com/pin/1234567890) — this is the URL to a certain Pin and represents the format needed by PinViddl.
  • Profile URL (e.g. pinterest.com/username/) — goes to the profile, not to one unique Pin.
  • Board URL (e.g. pinterest.com/username/board-name/) — this is the address for a board containing many Pins. There is no way to download a video with this link.
  • Search URL (e.g. pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=...) — the user will obtain a page with search results, but not the Pin itself.
  • Pin share link (e.g. pin.it/xxxxxx) — these links are generated when the user clicks the "Share" button. Typically, these links redirect to the Pin page, allowing the users to download the video as long as the link is valid.

When in doubt about what kind of link it is, the first step is to open it in a browser. If the page displays one video, along with one description and comment section, then that Pin page is the link you want.

Why Isn't My Pinterest Video Downloading?

There are several causes for a download to fail, and most do not depend on PinViddl:

  • Incorrect link type. You can't download a link to a board, a profile, or a search. Return to the particular Pin.
  • The Pin was deleted. If the original Pin was deleted, it can no longer be accessed.
  • The Pin is private or has restricted access. Pins which are private or only shared with select followers cannot be used if you are not logged in to Pinterest. PinViddl can download public Pins only.
  • The Pin does not contain any video. There are Pins that look like they contain a video, but in fact they are either pictures or GIFs. Check the Pin on Pinterest — if it cannot play as a video, it cannot be downloaded.
  • The source of the video cannot be opened. Sometimes a Pin indicates a video that cannot be opened, even though the Pin is public.
  • Mistake when copying and pasting the URL. If only part of the URL was copied from the share menu, PinViddl would not be able to find the Pin.
  • A temporary connection issue. Poor network connection or an outdated browser might cause your problem. Reloading the page and trying again usually resolves it.

Not every video can be downloaded from Pinterest. Downloading capabilities depend on how the Pin was made public, not on repeated attempts.
