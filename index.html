What Pinviddl Actually Does

Pinterest embeds video directly into its Pin pages, but it doesn't give you a save button for those videos. Pinviddl fills that gap. Paste the Pin URL, and Pinviddl extracts the video stream from the public page — no login, no Pinterest account, no browser extension required.

The result is a clean MP4 you can keep on your phone or computer. Workout clips, cooking tutorials, DIY walk-throughs, travel reels — if it plays as a video on a public Pinterest Pin, Pinviddl can pull it.

Getting the Right Pinterest Link for Video

Pinterest video lives inside a Pin, not a board or a profile. The URL you need ends with pinterest.com/pin/ followed by a numeric ID, or it's a shortened pin.it/ link from the app's share menu — both point to the same place.

The most common mistake is copying a board URL while a Pin is open inside it. The address bar sometimes still shows the board address rather than the Pin. To be certain, tap or click the Pin so it opens as its own full page, then copy the link. That's the one Pinviddl needs.

Saving Pinterest Videos on Android and iPhone

On Android, open the Pinterest app, find the video Pin, tap the share arrow, and hit Copy Link. Switch to Chrome or your preferred browser, navigate to Pinviddl, and paste. Once the video preview loads, tap the download button — Chrome usually drops MP4s straight into your Downloads folder.

On iPhone, the flow is the same through the Pinterest app, but Safari handles downloads differently: the MP4 may open in a preview player first. Press and hold the screen, then choose Save to Files or Share → Save Video depending on your iOS version. Either route gets the file onto your device.

Public Pins Only — Why That Matters

Pinviddl reads the same page a regular visitor would see. If a Pin's video is visible to anyone on the internet without a Pinterest login, Pinviddl can process it. If it's behind a private board, restricted to specific followers, or has been taken down, there's nothing on the public page to read — and nothing for Pinviddl to return.

This is by design, not a bug. The tool doesn't log in to Pinterest on your behalf or attempt to bypass access controls. It works exclusively with publicly accessible content.

Pinterest Video Quality — What to Expect

Pinviddl retrieves the video at the resolution Pinterest makes available for that Pin. Many Pins serve 720p or 1080p video; some older or lower-quality uploads may only have a smaller version. The tool doesn't re-encode or compress anything — what you download is exactly what Pinterest's servers are serving for that video at the time you run it.

If multiple quality options are detected, they appear as separate download choices so you can pick the one that suits your storage or use case.

Fixing a Failed Pinterest Video Download

When a video doesn't come through, one of these is almost always the cause:

  • Board or profile URL instead of a Pin URL. Open the Pin directly — its own page, not inside a board view — and copy the address from there.
  • The Pin requires a login. Private boards and follower-only content can't be accessed publicly, so Pinviddl can't reach them either.
  • The Pin no longer exists. Deleted or expired Pins leave nothing for Pinviddl to retrieve, regardless of which downloader you use.
  • Pinterest recently updated its page structure. Pinviddl is updated to match Pinterest's changes, but there can be a brief gap after a major platform update. Try again in a few hours if this seems to be the case.

Copyright and Fair Use

Every video on Pinterest belongs to the person or organisation that uploaded it. Downloading a copy for personal reference — saving a recipe video, keeping a workout routine offline — is the intended use case for Pinviddl.

Reuploading someone else's video to another platform, using it in a commercial project, or presenting it as your own work is a different matter entirely. If that's the plan, reach out to the creator and get explicit permission before publishing. Pinviddl doesn't grant any rights beyond what you already have as a viewer of a public Pin.
